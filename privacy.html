Summary

We designed this app to work offline and to minimise data collection. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.

Data we process

  • Study progress (on device): answers, scores, bookmarks, streaks, exam date, daily goals and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (e.g. UserDefaults / app sandbox on iOS, app-private SharedPreferences on Android).
  • Purchases: subscription and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may share limited purchase/entitlement status with the app to unlock features. On Android, purchases are processed by Google Play Billing; Google shares only the purchase status the app needs to unlock features, and we never receive your payment details.
  • Optional notifications: if you enable a daily study reminder, the system notification framework schedules a local notification on your device. We do not operate a remote push server for this app.
  • Support email: if you email us, we receive the content of your message and your email address to respond.

What we do not do

  • No third-party advertising
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal data
  • No account system requiring name, medical history, or clinical patient data

Health & medical information

The app provides educational exam-prep content only. It is not a medical device and does not provide personalised medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not enter patient-identifiable clinical data into the app.

Children

The app is intended for adult learners preparing for professional exams, not for children under 13.

Data retention & deletion

Local study data remains on your device until you delete the app or use in-app reset progress. To request deletion of any support email correspondence, contact us at the address above.

International users

The App Store may process transactions in your region under Apple’s terms, and Google Play under Google’s terms. Our support inbox may receive messages from any country.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
