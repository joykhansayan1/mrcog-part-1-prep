Common topics

Restore purchases

Open the paywall or Settings → Restore Purchases, and sign in with the same Apple ID used to buy. On Android, make sure Google Play is signed in to the Google account you bought with, then tap Restore Purchases.

Manage or cancel subscription

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Study data

Progress is stored on your device. Reset is available in Settings. Deleting the app removes local data.
